Build It Your Way

Deep and flexible build systems that let you shape how you and your Spirit fight.

  • ~550 nodes per hero, and per Spirit
  • Keystones that change how a skill plays
  • Attributes and Training: shape your stats
  • Status builds: bleed, burn, poison and more
  • Swap Spirits, re-spec, experiment

Gear That Matters

Find, craft and enhance gear that defines your build.

  • 5 gear tiers, 18 sets with build-changing bonuses
  • Main stats and random substats
  • Enhance to +15, reforge, craft and salvage at the Blacksmith
  • Sigils: passive powers that bend your build
